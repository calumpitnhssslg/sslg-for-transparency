WHO WE ARE

Student leadership
starts with service.

The Supreme Secondary Learner Government is a student-led organization that represents the learners of Calumpit National High School. It provides students with opportunities to participate, lead, and contribute to the school community.

OUR PURPOSE

Lead. Serve. Represent.

🎯

Leadership

Encourage students to develop leadership skills and take an active role in school activities.

🤝

Service

Promote activities and initiatives that help improve the student and school community.

🗣️

Representation

Give learners a platform to express their ideas, concerns, and suggestions.

🌟

Student Participation

Encourage students to participate in meaningful school programs and activities.

OUR COMMITMENT

For the students, by the students.

The SSLG strives to listen to the learners of Calumpit National High School and help turn meaningful ideas into action.
