Mabuhay ang Batang Lider!

Welcome to the official website of the Supreme Secondary Learner Government of Calumpit National High School.

As the student-led governing body of the school, the SSLG strives to represent learners, promote student participation, and create meaningful programs that contribute to the development of the school community.

Through service, leadership, collaboration, and responsibility, we aim to empower every learner to become an active and responsible member of the school community.
